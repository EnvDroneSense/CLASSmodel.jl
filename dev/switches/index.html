Switches

Only sw_ml is on by default. Switch the other components on in the input.

SwitchComponent
sw_mlmixed-layer growth, entrainment and tendencies (default on)
sw_shearweshear contribution to entrainment
sw_fixftfree troposphere fixed under subsidence
sw_windprognostic wind
sw_slsurface layer: ustar, drag coefficients, 2 m diagnostics
sw_radradiation: solar angle, net radiation
sw_lsland surface, with ls_type = :js (Jarvis-Stewart) or :ags (A-Gs, needs sw_rad)
sw_seawith sw_ls: open water at the fixed Ts instead of land. G and LEref are NaN
sw_cushallow cumulus: mass flux and cloud core fraction
sw_curadwith sw_cu and sw_rad: the cloud cover of the radiation is 2 ac of the previous step
sw_wtheta, sw_wqdiurnal cycle of the prescribed fluxes (see below)
sw_chemgas-phase chemistry of the 22 species (see Species and chemistry)
x = ModelInput(; sw_rad = true, sw_sl = true, sw_ls = true, ls_type = :ags, c3c4 = :c4)
out = run_model(x)
(H = maximum(out.H), LE = maximum(out.LE), wCO2A = minimum(out.wCO2A))
(H = 168.86380653193604, LE = 92.5185191932523, wCO2A = -0.5163954825014517)

Diurnal cycle of the surface fluxes

Without sw_ls, wtheta and wq are constant. With sw_wtheta or sw_wq, the flux is the input value times sin(pi t / sinperiod), with t in seconds from the start of the run. sinperiod is the half period of the sine [s]. The default is 43200 s.

x = ModelInput(; sw_wtheta = true, sw_wq = true, sinperiod = 12 * 3600)
out = run_model(x)
maximum(out.wtheta)
